\begin{changelogentry}{2025-11-02}{R. Totake}{1.0.0}
Initial release of AIM-ALPHA documentation.
\end{changelogentry}
\begin{changelogentry}{2026-2-11}{R. Totake}{1.0.1}
Added protected areas; split forests into primary and secondary categories; and divided pastureland into grazed and ungrazed categories to more accurately represent land use and grazing intensity.
\end{changelogentry}
\begin{changelogentry}{2026-2-21}{R. Totake}{1.0.2}
Added an optional SSP-based urban land projection extension using the SEDAC 1-km urban dataset, while keeping the LUH2-consistent base-year urban area as the default.
\end{changelogentry}
\begin{changelogentry}{2026-2-27}{R. Totake}{1.0.3}
Updated scenario and inter-model integration settings: introduced split scenario controls (\texttt{socioeconomic\_setting}, \texttt{climatepolicy\_setting}, \texttt{climate\_setting}), standardized scenario suffix control with \texttt{ModelInt}, changed \texttt{runlist} format to \texttt{socioeconomic|climatepolicy|climate}, and added optional pre-run bridge processes (\texttt{hubauto}/Hub2ALPHA and \texttt{afoluauto}/AFOLU2ALPHA).
\end{changelogentry}
\begin{changelogentry}{2026-08-29}{R. Totake}{1.0.4}
Updated the land-use and afforestation formulation, protected-area constraints, crop-yield drivers, inter-model integration, and model settings.
\end{changelogentry}
\begin{changelogentry}{2026-10-18}{R. Totake}{1.0.5}
Standardized model symbol names and documentation, added development guidelines, and updated the regression scripts and figures.
Updated price, subsidy and tax formulations, crop yields and production costs, forest and afforestation constraints, protected areas, price-data gap filling, and land-data processing.
\end{changelogentry}
\begin{changelogentry}{2026-10-19}{R. Totake}{1.0.6}
Added the fishery sector: capture fisheries and aquaculture for fish and aquatic products, reduction fish as the raw material of fishmeal and fish oil, and aquaculture feed demand, with data processing based on FishStat and Naylor et~al.~(2021). Added fishery products to the AgMIP and IAMC outputs.
\end{changelogentry}
